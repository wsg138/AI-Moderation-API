  names/game terms, and obvious English-dominant chat with a small familiar

  choose REVIEW instead of guessing from non-ASCII letters or script.

  flirtation and some picture requests can be ALLOW when no actual coercion,

  prohibited slurs, targeted threats and credible severe abuse are still

* Decide message visibility separately from speaker blame. Staff only decides
